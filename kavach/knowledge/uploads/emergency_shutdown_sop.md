# Emergency Shutdown SOP

## Purpose
Defines the procedure for an emergency shutdown of plant equipment.

## Trigger Conditions
- Reactor pressure exceeds 12.5 bar.
- Coolant flow drops below 40 litres per minute.

## Shutdown Sequence
1. The operator must press the Emergency Stop within 10 seconds of the alarm.
2. Isolate the feed valve within 2 minutes.
3. Notify the Shift Supervisor immediately.

## Restart Authorization
Restart requires written sign-off from the Plant Safety Officer, and may not
occur less than 6 hours after an emergency shutdown.
